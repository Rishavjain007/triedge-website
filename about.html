About TRIEDGE – Where Logistics Meets Material Innovation

TRIEDGE was founded to solve a unique industry challenge: how to manufacture advanced nano materials while ensuring their safe, efficient transport worldwide. Today, we operate two integrated divisions – Transport & Logistics and Advanced Materials Manufacturing – under one roof.

Our transport division handles road freight across Europe and sea freight to global destinations, including temperature‑controlled options for sensitive nano powders. Our manufacturing facility produces high‑purity nano zinc oxide, nano silver oxide, nano silica, and proprietary antibacterial/antiviral blends.
