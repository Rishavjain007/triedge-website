Our Process

Structured, Proven Methodology for Project Success

Our Proven Process Methodology

TRIEDGE follows a structured, time-tested process that ensures successful project delivery, quality outcomes, and client satisfaction. Our methodology is flexible enough to accommodate various project types while maintaining rigor and consistency.

01

Requirement Analysis

Duration: 1-2 weeks

We begin by thoroughly understanding your specific needs, constraints, objectives, and success criteria. This phase is critical in setting the foundation for project success.

Activities Include:
  • Stakeholder interviews and kickoff meetings
  • Business requirement documentation
  • Technical requirement specification
  • Scope definition and boundary setting
  • Risk and constraint identification
  • Success metrics and KPI definition
Deliverables:
  • Requirements Document
  • Project Charter
  • Scope Statement
  • Risk Register
02

Planning & Design

Duration: 2-4 weeks

Based on the requirements, we develop a comprehensive plan and detailed design that serves as the roadmap for execution. Our designs are validated and optimized before moving forward.

Activities Include:
  • Detailed project planning and scheduling
  • Resource allocation and team structuring
  • Technical design and architecture development
  • Engineering design and drawings
  • Budget estimation and financial planning
  • Communication and stakeholder management plan
Deliverables:
  • Project Plan and Schedule
  • Technical Specifications
  • Engineering Drawings and CAD Models
  • Budget and Resource Plan
  • Communication Plan
03

Execution

Duration: Varies by project (weeks to months)

Our dedicated team executes the project according to the approved plan. We maintain strict adherence to timelines, quality standards, and budget allocations with regular monitoring and adaptive management.

Activities Include:
  • Implementation per approved plan
  • Equipment procurement and installation
  • System integration and configuration
  • Regular progress monitoring and reporting
  • Change management and scope control
  • Issue and risk management
Deliverables:
  • Installed and Configured Systems
  • Weekly Progress Reports
  • Updated Project Documentation
  • Issue and Change Logs
04

Testing & Quality Assurance

Duration: 1-3 weeks

Comprehensive testing and quality assurance ensures that all deliverables meet specification requirements and performance standards. We validate functionality, safety, and compliance.

Activities Include:
  • Functional testing and validation
  • Performance testing and optimization
  • Safety and compliance verification
  • User acceptance testing
  • Documentation review and finalization
  • Defect identification and rectification
Deliverables:
  • Test Report and Sign-off
  • Performance Validation Report
  • Compliance Certificate
  • Final Documentation and Manuals
05

Delivery & Support

Duration: Ongoing

Final delivery marks the beginning of our partnership. We ensure smooth handover, provide training, and offer comprehensive ongoing support to ensure sustained success and value creation.

Activities Include:
  • System handover and knowledge transfer
  • User training and documentation
  • Go-live support and monitoring
  • Issue resolution and troubleshooting
  • Performance monitoring and optimization
  • Ongoing maintenance and support plans
Deliverables:
  • Final Delivery Report
  • Training Materials
  • Support and Maintenance Plan
  • Performance Dashboard
  • 24/7 Support Helpdesk Access
